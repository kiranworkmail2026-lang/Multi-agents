# Acme Marketing — Brand Guidelines (v3, 2026)

## Voice
- **Tone:** confident, plain-spoken, specific. Never hype, never fluff.
- **Person:** we write in first-person plural ("we") for Acme, second-person ("you") for the customer.
- **Reading level:** 8th grade. No MBA jargon ("synergy", "leverage", "ideate") unless in a direct quote.
- **Humor:** dry, understated, occasional. Never slapstick or meme-chasing.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Proof over promise.** Every claim ships with a number, a customer name, or a working demo.
2. **Builders, not buyers.** Our audience is practitioners (PMs, engineers, analysts), not executives. Write to someone who has to ship on Monday.
3. **Open by default.** We cite sources, publish pricing, and link to our docs from the homepage.

## Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Cut lead-qualification time from 12 min to 3 min." | "Revolutionize your funnel with AI-native workflows." |
| Name the customer: "how Ramp uses Acme…" | Vague proof: "used by leading enterprises" |
| Publish benchmarks with methodology | Vanity stats without sources |

## Channel personalities
- **LinkedIn:** thought-leadership, practitioner POV, founder-written posts. No "excited to announce" openers.
- **Email (newsletter):** one idea per send, 300–500 words, always ships with one action.
- **Meta / Instagram:** product clips, behind-the-scenes, customer wins. Not our strongest channel — keep spend disciplined.
- **TikTok:** experimental tier. Short demos, dry humor. Do not allocate >5% of budget here until a clear CAC signal emerges.
- **Google Ads:** high-intent bottom-of-funnel keywords only. We do not bid on competitor brand terms.

## Budget principles
- Email and LinkedIn are the top-ROI channels historically — protect their budgets first.
- Meta and TikTok are always on a "test & learn" leash. Cut ruthlessly if ROI < 3x in any quarter.
- Google Ads budget scales with pipeline demand, not campaign count.

## Forbidden
- No fear-based marketing.
- No fake urgency ("only 3 left!").
- No stock photos of diverse people smiling at laptops.
- No AI-generated faces in creative.
