# Acme — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Who we sell to
- **Company size:** Series B to Series D, 200–2000 employees.
- **Industry:** B2B SaaS, fintech infrastructure, devtools, data platforms.
- **Geos:** US, UK, Germany, Netherlands, Singapore. Canada emerging.
- **Tech sophistication:** high. They have a platform team, a data team, and ship multiple times a day.

## Buyer persona — "Priya the Platform PM"
- Role: Senior PM or staff-level IC on platform/infrastructure teams.
- Goals: ship internal tools fast, reduce on-call load, measurable productivity gains.
- Pains: analytics tools that are too generic; dashboards no one uses; buying committee drag.
- Channel affinity: LinkedIn (high), Substack newsletters (high), podcasts (medium), Meta/TikTok (very low).
- Trigger events: new platform lead hire; post-incident retro; annual planning cycle.

## Buyer persona — "Dan the Data Lead"
- Role: Head of Data / Director of Analytics.
- Goals: unify metrics across tools; kill 5 SaaS subscriptions with one.
- Pains: data contracts break every sprint; exec pressure for ROI reports; vendor sprawl.
- Channel affinity: LinkedIn, technical blog posts, benchmark reports. Allergic to vendor webinars.
- Trigger events: renewal season for existing analytics tools; new CDO.

## Anti-ICP (do not pursue)
- Companies <50 employees (too small to benefit).
- Marketing-led orgs where platform is not a first-class function.
- Industries with strict data residency (healthcare, defense) until our EU-sovereign region ships.
- Buyers who ask for "all-in-one, no code, no engineering required."

## What they read
- Hacker News, Lenny's Newsletter, dbt Slack, Reforge, Pragmatic Engineer, The Pragmatic Programmer.
- They do NOT read Forbes, HBR think-pieces, or anything that looks like a press release.

## What converts them
- Benchmark reports with methodology.
- Founder-written LinkedIn posts with specific numbers.
- A 10-minute live demo with their actual data.
- A referral from another platform lead they respect.
