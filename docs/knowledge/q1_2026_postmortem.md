# Q1 2026 Marketing Post-Mortem

## Summary
Q1 hit 112% of pipeline target. Email and LinkedIn drove 68% of attributed revenue on 25% of spend. Meta and TikTok underperformed for the third consecutive quarter.

## What worked
- **Email newsletter "Builder's Edge"** — added a weekly benchmark segment. Open rate 41% (prev 28%), click rate 9.2%. Drove 520+ conversions/month at ~$0.50 CAC.
- **LinkedIn founder posts** — Neha's "why we killed our MQL dashboard" post drove 41k impressions and 14 inbound demo requests. Reproduce this pattern.
- **Google Ads — bottom-of-funnel only** — narrowing to 18 high-intent keywords (from 120) lifted Google ROI from 3.2x to 5.4x.

## What didn't
- **Meta Ads** — despite $15.7k spend, ROI stuck at 2.56x. Creative fatigue is real; we rotated assets 3x and conversion rate kept sliding. Hypothesis: our ICP (technical practitioners) doesn't convert from Meta.
- **TikTok** — $6k burned, ROI 2.5x. Demo-style content didn't translate. Pausing for Q2 unless a new angle emerges.
- **Webinars** — ran 3 in Q1, avg 42 attendees, 2 deals. Too expensive to produce for the yield. Defer until we have a big-name co-host.

## Decisions carried forward
1. Shift 40% of Meta budget to LinkedIn starting Q2.
2. Pause TikTok paid spend; keep organic experimentation at <$500/month.
3. Grow email list by 25% via gated benchmark reports (not ebooks).
4. Do NOT bid on competitor brand keywords. Stance unchanged.

## Open questions
- Is Meta salvageable with a completely different creative direction (founder POV instead of product demo)?
- Can we build a LinkedIn influencer partnership program without diluting the "builders, not buyers" voice?
- Email list growth is our single biggest lever — what's the ceiling?
